Careful surface preparation and compatible coatings for siding, masonry, stucco, and exterior trim.

Service overview

A durable finish begins with a sound surface.

We assess existing paint, weather exposure, and surface condition before recommending a coating system. Cleaning, removal of loose paint, repairs, and priming help the finish adhere evenly and protect your exterior.

  • Siding, stucco, masonry, doors, and trim
  • Protection for windows, landscaping, and pathways
  • Weather-aware scheduling and tidy handover
What we do

Care at every stage.

Surface Preparation

Cleaning, scraping loose coatings, sanding, and minor surface repairs.

Exterior Coatings

Compatible primers and finish coats selected for the surface and exposure.

Trim & Details

Careful finishing around doors, windows, fascia, and exterior trim.

Our process

A clear plan from assessment to handover.

What affects cost

Your quotation reflects the work required.

Surface area, building height, access, existing paint condition, repairs, and the coating system all affect your quotation.

We confirm the scope after reviewing your space and provide a written quotation covering preparation, materials, and application.
