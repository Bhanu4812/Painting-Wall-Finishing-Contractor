Waterproofing starts with understanding the moisture.

Surface assessment, careful detailing, and compatible waterproofing systems for terraces, balconies, and exposed walls.

Service overview

Address the source and protect vulnerable details.

We inspect visible moisture damage, surface condition, drainage, joints, and penetrations before proposing work. The selected system is applied to a prepared substrate, with special attention to corners and transitions where water can enter.

  • Terraces, balconies, and exposed exterior surfaces
  • Careful treatment of joints, corners, and penetrations
  • System-specific curing and maintenance guidance

What affects cost

Your quotation reflects the work required.

Surface area, substrate condition, drainage issues, access, detailing requirements, and the selected waterproofing system affect the estimate.

We confirm the scope after reviewing your space and provide a written quotation covering preparation, materials, and application.
