What affects cost

Your quotation reflects the actual work required.

Room size matters, but wall condition, access, number of colors, finish type, trim detail, required repairs, and product selection can matter just as much.

We do not promise a fixed price without understanding the space. Every written quote separates preparation, materials, and application.

For an accurate estimate

  • Share clear room and damage photos
  • Provide approximate room dimensions
  • Tell us preferred colors and sheen
  • Share access and timing needs
